    What does no win, no fee mean for an RAF claim?+

    You pay no legal fee unless your claim succeeds. If it does, our fee is a share of what you receive, limited by the Contingency Fees Act, and explained in writing before you sign.

    How long do I have to claim from the RAF?+

    Usually three years from the date of the accident, and two years if the driver was never identified. Contact us early, as medical and police records take time to collect.

    Can I claim against a government hospital?+

    Yes. Claims are brought against the provincial health department, and a formal notice must usually be sent within six months. Different rules can apply to children.

    A family member has been arrested. What should I do?+

    Call us straight away on 039 976 1089. An arrested person must be brought before a court within 48 hours, which is usually when bail is decided.

    I was dismissed. Is it too late to challenge it?+

    An unfair dismissal dispute generally has to be referred to the CCMA within 30 days. Speak to us as soon as possible.
